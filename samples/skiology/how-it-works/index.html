01

Tell us who's skiing.

Names, adult or youth, and whether you want the Rossignol rental, a demo, or the adult season lease. The shop pulls from that note. If you already know a boot size, put it in the reservation.

02

We set the gear.

Daily rentals are Rossignol skis and Nordica boots. Demos are Kästle, Fischer, and Elan when those skis are in. Shawn's bench is a Wintersteiger, if a tune is part of the visit.

03

Park at the door. Show the pass.

Access from Killington Road or West Hill Road, parking just steps from the door. The pass has your code, the party, and a QR the desk can read. Boots get a fit before anyone walks out.
