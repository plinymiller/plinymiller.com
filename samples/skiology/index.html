The shop

A small crew on the Access Road.

We're a small but dedicated bunch. Skiology is owned and operated by longtime Killington residents Shawn Phelps and Barbara Caraher.

Shawn has spent many years managing ski shops as well as ski tuning and buying. He knows everything there is to know about skis and ski tuning. Barb spent years buying for shops in Killington and on Long Island. She has an eye for fashion, and she pays close attention to value.

Located just shy of a mile up the Killington Access Road, at Killington Road and West Hill Road — just before the stoplight. Park at the door. The resort is a few minutes on.

How pickup works

On the wall and on the bench

Rentals, demos, tunes, and the season lease.

  • Daily rentals

    High quality Rossignol rental skis and comfortable Nordica boots, for adults and youth. Poles come with the set.

    Call for the rate

  • Demos

    High performance demo skis from Kästle, Fischer, and Elan, subject to availability.

    Call the shop

  • Adult season lease

    Skis, boots, and poles. Adults only. November 1 through April 15.

    $299

  • Wintersteiger tuning

    Race tune, hot box, base repair, stone grind, edge and wax. The Season Tune covers minor base repair, a stone grind, and edge and wax from November 1 to April 15. It pays for itself after three tunes. Each person may put three pairs on the program, one pair a week. Excessive base damage is extra.

    Call for the tune

  • Home drop-off

    For an additional fee, freshly tuned skis can be dropped at home. Limited service area.

    Call 802-422-SKIS

  • Race center

    Alpine race gear for U12, U14, U16, U19, PG, and Academy athletes. Slalom and giant slalom from Fischer and Rossignol. Fischer RC4 65 and Fischer RC4 Podium LT 70 are on the board.

    $99 / $149

Daily rental prices are not posted. Call 802-422-SKIS (7547) for the rate or to reserve. Or hold a demo pass online and confirm the number at the door.
